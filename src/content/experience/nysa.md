## The work

I gathered requirements from stakeholders for an ed-tech platform and turned them into structured SRS and FRS documents before any code was written. The engineering team was working against a deadline, so the goal was writing requirements specific enough that an engineer could point to exactly where they disagreed.

## What I learned

This is still how I like to start a project: figure out what was actually asked for, what it actually means, and what's explicitly out of scope.
