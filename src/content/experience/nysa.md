## The work

I gathered client requirements for an ed-tech platform and turned them into structured SRS and FRS documentation. The engineering team was on a clock; the useful skill was not a framework, it was writing something a builder could disagree with precisely.

## What it trained

Requirements work is still how I start: what did they actually ask for, what did they mean, and what will we refuse to pretend is in scope.
