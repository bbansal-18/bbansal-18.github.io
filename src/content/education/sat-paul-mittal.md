My high school experiences played a pivotal role in shaping who I am today. Being Head Boy for two years taught me what responsibility actually looks like in practice. It was not about taking on every burden myself or offloading it onto others; it was about managing and organizing logistics well enough that the student body could function smoothly without either happening. Creating a platform where students could actually voice their concerns opened my eyes to issues I had never noticed before. Being able to help resolve some of those issues was one of the more quietly fulfilling parts of the role.

In my senior year, I was elected President of Satyan Innovation Fest 2023, a national-level hackathon for high schoolers building tech-driven solutions for large-scale social impact. It was a fast, high-pressure role — meeting dignitaries and investors, coordinating logistics, contacting vendors, and making sure the event actually ran the way it was supposed to. It taught me how to operate under real time pressure, with real stakeholders, and how to make quick decisions when there wasn't room to overthink them.

Academically, I finished third in Ludhiana in Class 10, with 98.4% in ICSE board examinations, and was later named valedictorian and state topper in Class 12, with 98.8% in ISC board examinations. None of that happened by myself, as my parents pushed me to consistently perform at my best and gave me the strength and grounding to keep sharpening my skills, even when it was hard.

At commencement, being named valedictorian meant I got to give the graduating speech with a chance to look back at the last 14 years at this school and try to leave something behind for the peers and juniors still finding their own way. I centered it around our school motto, "Learning Without Limits," and what it actually means to keep striving for excellence long after the exams are over.
