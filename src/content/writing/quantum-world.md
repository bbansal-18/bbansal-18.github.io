## About the book

The book explains quantum mechanics to a novice: how particles behave at the smallest scales, and how that underlies the physics we experience day to day. It covers core quantum theory, a qualitative walk through quantum field theory and string theory, the basics of relativity, and how physicists arrived at the idea of quantum gravity.

## Why I wrote it

The explanations I wanted as a student were either too thin or buried in equations before the ideas made sense. I kept it qualitative on purpose, using analogies to do the work a first course would normally do for someone without formal mathematical knowledge.

## Release and Sales

Notion Press published it in March 2022; it's sold more than 500 copies. Padma Shri Dr. P. S. Goel, a former director at ISRO, released it at the International Conference on Small Satellites 2022 at LPU, Punjab.
