I wrote *The Quantum World* because the explanations I wanted as a student were either too thin or too proud of their equations. The book is qualitative on purpose: core quantum theory, a walk through quantum field theory and string theory as ideas, the basics of relativity, and how the quantum-gravity question got named. Analogies do the work that a first course would do with operators.

Notion Press published it in March 2022. More than 300 copies have sold. Padma Shri Dr. P. S. Goel, former director at ISRO, released it at the International Conference on Small Satellites 2022 at LPU.

It is a high-school book. I still like that it exists: a complete object, with a spine, that I had to finish.
