## Why it existed

Fetching water is a load you wear on your body. Jal Vahini started from that fact, not from a framework. We co-led the design of ergonomic water rollers for families in rural Rajasthan and iterated on prototypes until they were something we were willing to give away.

## What we did

We partnered with local manufacturers and academic institutions in Hanumangarh to scale production and distribution. The work was physical: prototyping, donation, and the logistics of making more than one.

## Why it is still on this site

Not every project that matters is a repository. This one is here because it is part of the same habit as the clinical software: build the thing the people in front of you can use.
