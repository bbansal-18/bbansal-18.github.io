## Why it existed

Carrying water is physical work that mostly falls on women and children in rural households. Jal Vahini started from that directly: we co-designed ergonomic water rollers for families in rural Rajasthan and iterated on prototypes until we were confident enough to give them away.

## What we did

We partnered with local manufacturers and academic institutions in Hanumangarh to scale production and distribution. Most of the work was physical — prototyping, donation logistics, and figuring out how to make more than a handful of units.

## Why it is still on this site

This wasn't a coding project, but it comes from the same instinct as my software work: build something the people can actually use and benefit from.
